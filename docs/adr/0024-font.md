# ADR-0024: Primary Mushaf font — KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs v2.2; Amiri Quran as documented alternate

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Deciders:** dahoumanekhalil
- **Review date:** at Phase 2 rendering acceptance (TASK-029)

## Context and problem statement

The reader's typography is the app's visual center (charter — Content > Reading Comfort > everything else). We must pick a Mushaf-quality Uthmani font that:

- Covers every codepoint present in the frozen Quran corpus (70 distinct codepoints in `data/output/intermediate/unicode-profile.md`).
- Reproduces the visual character of a printed Madani mushaf.
- Has a license that allows bundling in a free Android app distributed on Google Play (see charter #1, #2, #3).

This ADR selects the **font file**; the packaging strategy (bundled asset) is governed by ADR-0015.

## Decision drivers

- **Authenticity of the printed Madani mushaf reference** (charter #8 — content integrity extends to typographic form).
- **Complete coverage of our corpus's 70 codepoints** (verified programmatically against the font's `cmap`).
- **License compatibility with a free, non-commercial app**.
- **Install size** (fonts are small compared to the DB; ~200-500 KB each is easily acceptable).
- **Long-term availability** (not tied to a single fragile vendor URL).

## Options considered

### Option A — KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs v2.2 (recommended primary)

The typeface used to typeset the physical Madani mushaf, produced by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex. General-purpose text font (not the per-page bitmap-style QPC fonts).

- **Coverage:** all 70 corpus codepoints present. `cmap` has 608 entries.
- **File:** `uthmanic_hafs_v22.ttf`, 297,688 B, SHA-256 `a6e59510dcaf3ec9...`.
- **License:** "ELECTRONIC END-USER LICENSE AGREEMENT" embedded in the font's `name` table (extracted verbatim to `LICENSES/KFGQPC-EULA.txt`). Grants: **Use, Copy, Distribute — free of cost.** Forbids: Sell, Modify, Alter, Translate, Reverse-Engineer, Decompile.
- **Compatible with our use?** Yes — we ship unmodified, our app is free, we credit KFGQPC.
- **Sourcing:** upstream `http://fonts.qurancomplex.gov.sa/` intermittently reachable; we vendored via the well-known mirror `github.com/nuqayah/qpc-fonts` at path `text-mushafs/UthmanicHafs_V22/uthmanic_hafs_v22.ttf`. The file itself carries the KFGQPC copyright and license in its `name` table, so provenance is self-verifiable.

### Option B — Amiri Quran v1.003 (recommended alternate)

Independent, freely-licensed Quran-focused variant of the Amiri family by Khaled Hosny.

- **Coverage:** all 70 corpus codepoints present. `cmap` has 266 entries.
- **File:** `AmiriQuran.ttf`, 136,920 B, SHA-256 `e2a47644762d16bd...`.
- **License:** SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`LICENSES/SIL-OFL-1.1.txt`). Grants: use, study, copy, merge, embed, modify, redistribute. Forbids: selling the font by itself. Very permissive; the safest possible license for a mobile app.
- **Compatible with our use?** Yes — no restrictions on our use case.

### Option C — Noto Naskh Arabic, Scheherazade New, or other general-purpose Arabic fonts

General-purpose Arabic, not designed for the Quran specifically. Coverage of Quranic annotation marks (small high seen, small high madda, superscript alef, sajdah symbol, rub-al-hizb) is thinner. Not chosen — using a non-Mushaf font would degrade the "digital Mushaf" quality bar.

## Decision

**Primary (MVP): KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs v2.2.**
**Documented alternate (may be ship-flagged in POST-MVP): Amiri Quran v1.003.**

KFGQPC is the printed-mushaf typeface itself — the strongest possible match to the visual reference our users know. Its license, though not OFL, is unambiguously compatible with our free, unmodified-redistribution model. Coverage exceeds Amiri (608 vs 266 cmap entries) which pays off on the long tail of less-common Quranic marks.

Amiri Quran is retained as an alternate because (a) its OFL license is a robust safety net if a licensing question ever arises, and (b) it exercises the "alternate font" code path when we add font selection in POST-MVP.

## Consequences

- Positive: the reader visually matches a physical Madani mushaf; both fonts are archived + hashed + licensed; alt-font pathway pre-exists.
- Negative — accepted:
  - Two fonts in the assets tree eventually (~440 KB total). Trivial.
  - KFGQPC's forbid-modification clause means we must ship the exact bytes, and any font-subsetting for install-size optimization is forbidden on the KFGQPC file. The Amiri file can be subset if we ever need to.
- Follow-up:
  - **NOTICE** and the About screen (Phase 7) must credit KFGQPC + Tanzil + quran-meta + Amiri.
  - Phase 2 TASK-026 uses the primary font for the rendering prototype; TASK-029 signs off or (unlikely) sends us back here.
  - Phase 15 (visual refinement) validates the type sizes on the reference device matrix.
